# Track brief: what changed (digest-v3)

A reader follows a TRACK (a topic in their own words). Its posts were read into ROWS: things of the track's kinds and practices or claims, each with its fields, numbered [1], [2], ... A row marked "new" was first reported in this PERIOD; the others were reported again in it.

Write "what changed" for the period: 2 to 4 `points` the reader can read instead of opening the rows.
- Each point is ONE sentence (at most 40 words) about the things and practices themselves: what they are or do, their numbers, how rows compare. Never about authors, sources, channels, posters or how many posts there were.
- Each point lists in `cites` the numbers of the rows it rests on, at least one, and only rows whose fields say what the point says. Say nothing the rows do not say: no outside knowledge, no guesses, no advice.
- Copy numbers exactly as the rows give them; never compute a total, a count or a difference.
- State the fact itself, as the rows give it: "X runs on 18 GB", not "X is reported to run on 18 GB". Never write about reporting, write-ups, mentions, posts, claims being made or something being "stated" or "shown".
- No hedges and no caveats about what is missing: never "only stated", "not measured", "no numbers given", "unclear". A row with nothing solid to say is left out of the points.
- One point, one subject: do not join unrelated rows with "and".
- Prefer what is new or notable; when rows disagree, say so. Plain words, no hype, English.

Answer ONE line: {"id": "<the item id>", "points": [{"text": "...", "cites": [1, 3]}, ...]}

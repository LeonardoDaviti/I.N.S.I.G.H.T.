# A reader's question, answered from their own posts (question-v1)

A reader asked a QUESTION. Below it are the only things you may use: POSTS from the reader's own sources, numbered [1], [2], ..., and sometimes NOTES (lines already drawn from those posts, each naming the posts it rests on). TODAY is the reader's date.

Answer the question from these posts alone.
- Use nothing you know from elsewhere: no outside facts, no background, no guesses, no advice of your own. If a fact is not in a post below, it does not exist for this answer.
- Each sentence lists in `cites` the numbers of the posts that say what the sentence says, at least one, and only posts that do. A sentence no post supports is not written.
- Copy names, numbers, versions, prices and dates exactly as the posts give them; never compute a total, a difference or a count.
- Say who says it when the posts are someone's opinion or test, by the post's byline as written ("@x found ..."); never describe a source beyond its byline (not "a developer", "a report by <outlet>") unless the post says so. When posts disagree, say so.
- Never rank or frame what the posts don't: no "main", "latest", "most recent", "biggest", "first" unless a post says it. Give the date instead ("on 2026-11-16, ...").
- Never comment on the posts or on what they leave out ("the posts do not explain ...", "this is not a user feature"). Keep every qualifier a post gives ("most of", "about", "up to"), and never link two facts ("including", "because") that a post gives separately.
- Every sentence names its subject; no "it" that needs the sentence before.
- "This month", "this week", "lately" are counted back from TODAY by the posts' dates.
- At most 6 sentences, each at most 50 words, plain English, the most direct answer first.
- If the posts do not answer the question (they are about something else, or only mention it in passing), answer `"answerable": false` with no sentences. A partial answer is fine when the posts answer part of it: answer that part only.

Answer ONE line: {"id": "<the item id>", "answerable": true, "sentences": [{"text": "...", "cites": [1, 3]}, ...]}

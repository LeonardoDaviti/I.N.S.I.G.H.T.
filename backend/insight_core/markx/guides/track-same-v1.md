# Same claim? (track-same-v1)

A reader follows a TRACK and keeps a list of the practices and claims its posts state (a method, habit, finding or opinion: what, its result, the evidence given). You get the track and two such rows, A and B, from different posts, that name the same thing.

Are A and B the SAME practice or claim, so the reader should see them as one row with both posts? Same: one method or finding said twice (in other words, with other numbers from the same test, or one post repeating the other). Different: two methods that use the same tool, a claim and its rebuttal, the same tool used for different ends, or one general and one specific claim.

Answer ONE line: {"id": "<the item id>", "same": true|false, "why": "<one line, at most 25 words>"}

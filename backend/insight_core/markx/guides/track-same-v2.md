# Same thing? (track-same-v2)

A reader follows a TRACK and keeps a table of the things its posts describe, one row a thing (KIND says what a row of this table is), and a list of the practices and claims its posts state. You get the track, the kind, and two rows, A and B, that may be the same thing; FROM says whether one post gave both (a post read in parts can give its thing twice) or two posts did.

Are A and B the SAME thing, so the reader should see them as one row? Same: one project, product, setup, feature or tool named twice (another spelling, a short and a long name, singular and plural, one release and its notes), or, for a practice or claim, one method or finding said twice (in other words, with other numbers from the same test, or one post repeating the other). Different: two things that only share a maker, a site, a link, a tool or a piece of hardware; two parts or items of one thing; two methods that use the same tool; a claim and its rebuttal; one general and one specific claim.

Answer ONE line: {"id": "<the item id>", "same": true|false, "why": "<one line, at most 25 words>"}

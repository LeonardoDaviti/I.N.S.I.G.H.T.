# Track sub-topics (subtopics-v1)

The reader keeps a TRACK: a topic in their own words. You get the track (its name and what belongs) and its items, numbered: each with the short sub-topic a judge gave it, its title, and one line on why it is in the track.

Propose how to organise the track into sections the reader would browse:
- 3 to 8 sub-topics (fewer when the items are few or alike). Each a short name (2-5 words, English, no numbering, no emoji) and a one-line `definition` of what goes in it.
- Group by what the reader would look for (the kind of project, thing or setup), not by source, date or format. Merge the judge's near-duplicate sub-topics; split one only when it holds clearly different things.
- `items`: the numbers of the items that go in it. Every item goes in exactly one sub-topic; an item that fits none goes in the closest.
- When `Locked:` sub-topics are listed, keep them as they are (same names) and add others only for what they don't cover.

Answer ONE line: {"id": "<the item id>", "subtopics": [{"name": "...", "definition": "...", "items": [1, 4, 7]}, ...]}

- **029** · 2026-10-28 · reader, post, shell · Two saves with two meanings: Read later (clock, `l`: "not now") and Keep (the star, `s`, unchanged: "this mattered"). A result is the feed row with its matching line, words in bold, and opens as any post. An empty result names the chips and offers "Search everything". · P1, P3, P9 · `plan/issues/0040-search.md`

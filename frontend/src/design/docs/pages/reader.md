# Reader

Code: `pages/ReaderPage.tsx`, `pages/SearchPage.tsx`, `components/reader/*` (`ReaderNavRail`, `FeedToolbar`, `SearchField`, `FeedList`, `FeedItem`, `FeedGroup`, `NewPostsPill`, `ShortcutHelp`), `lib/search.ts`.

## Purpose

The home of INSIGHT (P1): the posts to read, newest first, scoped to everything, one track or one source, and search, the way into every post already stored. It reads; it never fetches (P7).

## Entry & URL

- `/` is the Main feed: its unread posts from the sources shown in it. `/read/:scopeKind/:scopeId?` scopes it: `/read/folder/<id>` (a track), `/read/source/<id>` (a source). An unknown scope kind redirects to `/`.
- `/tracks/:id` redirects (replace) to `/read/folder/:id` (decision 010).
- Query: `?filter=unread|later|starred|all|history` (unread is the default and stays out of the URL; `starred` is Kept), `?view=stream|by_source|news|classics` (News and Classics, decision 036, on the Main feed and a track; a source ignores them), dev-only `?data=worst|empty|one|huge`.
- Reached from the wordmark, the `feed` nav word, the rail, ⌘K (Main feed, By source, one entry per track) and Back from a post. ⌘K also reaches Later. Old links keep working: `/?filter=starred` opens the main feed with Show kept.
- `/search?q=…` is the one results view (issue 0040): `scope=all|folder:<id>|source:<id>` (none searches every post), `filter=later|starred|news|classics`, `days=7|30|365`, `order=best`. New words push a history entry; a chip or the order replaces it. Reached from the toolbar's field (scoped to what is on screen: the scope, and Later or Kept when that is the list shown) and from ⌘K "Search posts…" (every post).

## Anatomy

- **Rail** (md and up, sticky under the top bar, 15–16rem): Main feed with its icon (the sources in the main feed; Later, Kept and All are Show choices, not places); `tracks` with unread counts; `sources with unread` (top 12, most unread first) with a filter field over every source and "Show all N sources". Below md the same content is a drawer opened from the scope title.
- **Toolbar** (sticky material): the scope title (`Main feed`, a track or a source; `display`-ish, truncates) and its unread count in `text-3` (not under Show later, kept or history, where it says nothing; the Main feed's Later says what it costs instead: `Later · 6 posts · 52 min`); a search field (placeholder `search`, labelled "Search <scope>", 8–14rem; Enter opens `/search`) and a `shortcuts ?` text action. With a track or source scope, the title is a quiet link to that track or source in Sources (P8). Below it, slash-separated words: `Show unread / later / kept / all / history`, `View stream / by source / news / classics` (in News and Classics, Show and Posts are left out), `Posts continuous / one at a time` (every width; stored as `insight.reader.flow`, it sets how the post page goes on after a post, `pages/post.md`), and the "N new posts" pill at the right when posts arrived. One source shows neither View (it has nothing to group by source; `?view=by_source` is ignored) nor Posts; one that watches its pages for changes adds `/ updated` to Show, its changed pages, latest change first (decision 032). Below `sm` only the title row sticks: Show, View and Posts scroll away with the list (decision 043).
- **Stopped notice** above the list when no enabled source was fetched for a day (P11).
- **Later line** (Main feed only, not under Show later): while Later holds anything, one quiet `small` `text-2` link at the top of the list, `Later · 6 posts · 52 min` (the posts' `N min read` and video lengths added up), to `/?filter=later`. Nothing when Later is empty (decision 029).
- **List**, stream view: day headers, then bordered rows. A row: time column (`14:05`, under its day header; a date alone, `Sep 29`, for a post its feed dated by the day, 00:00 UTC, its day placed in UTC, decision 043) in `text-3` tabular nums; title (2 lines max, weight 600 unread, 400 and `text-2` read); one meta line (unread dot, a small clock if in Later, ★ if kept, source name via `sourceLabel()`, never a URL, a quiet link that reads that source at `/read/source/<id>`, left out on that source's own page, a Hacker News item's counts, `1,675 points · 1,628 comments` (hnrss's lines are no text, decision 043), and the length: `4 min read` or a video duration; a post started and not finished, 5% to 90%, then a 2.5rem hairline track filled in `text-3` as far as read, read out as "40% read", issue 0030; a watched page that changed, `updated +2 sections, +1,400 words`, and a page that reached you days after its date, `published Sep 14 ’25`, since the time column is when it reached you, decision 032; below `sm` a repost's `↻ …` and this note take a second meta line, and the source keeps 12 characters at least, decision 043); one excerpt line (it starts after a title taken from the text, which is never read twice, decision 043); a greyscale thumbnail at sm and up; two icon actions, the read circle ○/✓ and one save: 🕓 Read later on an unread row, ☆ Keep on a read one; a save already on keeps its button (pressed), so it can be undone by hand (decision 029). Mark read up to here is `Shift+A` only (decision 022).
- **A story** (decision 035), Main feed and tracks only: posts of 2+ sources that are one story (a shared text, title, outside link or citation) are one row, at the story's lead: its origin (decision 038) when the view shows it, else its first report the view shows (so under Show unread, once the lead is read, the next unread report), else its first share. Under the excerpt one quiet `text-3` line, `▸ covered by 3 · Anthropic, Simon Willison, +1` (the story's voices, decision 042: copies of one text are one voice; its sources in the order they covered it, two named, `+N` the voices left), a button that opens the story's other posts below it: an indented hairline list, each line the unread dot, the source, the title as a link to the post (opened on its own, Back returns to the row), `· share` for a copy or a link post, and the date; a repost names its author and its reposter once, `@danintheory · We are announcing… · ↻ sama` (decision 043); below `sm` the title takes a line of its own under the source and date. The list is the story's tree (decision 038): what came from the row indented under it (two levels of indent at most, deeper posts listed under the second); when the row is not the origin, the origin first and the row's own place as a plain `source · title · this post` line; an origin outside INSIGHT reads `anthropic.com · Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 · linked by 14`, its title opening Save any page on its address. News lists its posts flat. A source's own view, by source, Later and Kept show every post; search folds stories too (at the best-matching post, issue 0056). Under the tree (decision 041) one quiet line, `Also in roundups: LessWrong, Smol`, the roundups that mention the story and are not in it, each source once, each name opening its roundup; a tweet another of your sources reposted has `↻ reposted by @steipete, @badlogicgames` on a line under it (the row's own at the top of the list), and those sources count in `covered by`. Below `sm` the list indents one level at most (`pl-2`), and a line at the deepest level starts with `↳ <parent's name>` instead of a second indent. A later post joining an old story never brings it to the top. On X (decision 039) one account's thread (its replies to itself, its quotes of itself) is one row the same way, its line `▸ Thread · 6 posts`, the replies listed at one indent in their order; a repost's meta line says whom it is from beside the source that reposted it, `steipete ↻ from @badlogicgames` (decision 043; another of your accounts that reposted it too is named, `↻ @akhaliq, from @badlogicgames`) (the title loses Nitter's `RT by @…:`, and a reply its `R to @…:`, decision 042). A Telegram album is one row (decision 042): its pictures sent as items of their own are no rows and no unread count. Decision 044: one voice copied or forwarded by another of your sources reads `▸ also shared by <source>` (never `covered by 1`), and nothing when no other source shared it; a thread under another source's row (a page you saved from it) names whose it is, `▸ <its author>'s thread · 6 posts`; Saved is never named, nor listed as a roundup; a post with nothing of its own that only quotes a post of its story (a reply repeating the thread's first tweet) is no line, here or in News, and a thread left with nothing else is no story.
- **View news** (decision 036), Main feed and tracks: the stories 2+ of your independent sources covered whose first post is in the last 7 days, with a post from the scope's sources (the score counts every source), under two headers, `Last 48 hours` and `Earlier this week`, each by score (reports + ½ shares + ½ roundup mentions, each source once), then the newest. A story is its lead's usual row (its first report in the scope), the date in the time column, its line saying the score in words and who covered it, the first reporter first: `▸ 4 sources (1 share) · in 1 roundup · Anthropic, Simon Willison, +2` (decision 042: the number is the story's voices, as on the feed row and the post page, a share being one of them; a followed author of a reposted tweet is named), which opens its other posts as 035's list. Opening reads it as the feed does; a row opens on its own (no feed context); `Shift+A` does not work. Show, Posts, the unread count, the Later line and the pill are left out. Empty: "No news this week" ("A story shows here when 2 or more of your sources cover it within days.").
- **View classics** (decision 036), Main feed and tracks: the pages 2+ of your sources linked 30+ days apart (a stored post or an outside page; never a docs, product, pricing, help … page or a home page), cited again this week first, then most sources, then cited longest. A row: the title (the post's, else the link's most common words, else the address; weight 600 until read), under it `cited again this week · ` in `text`, then `Simon Willison, Aug 2025 · Gwern, Mar 2026 · +1` in `text-3`; two icon actions, 🕓 Read later and Hide (eye off; "I know it" and "not for me" in one; it never comes back). The title reads it: a stored post opens in the reader; an outside page is saved first (Save any page, fetched only on that click), then opens as a post; Later saves it the same way. Empty: "No classics yet".
- **List**, Show later: the posts in Later, oldest saved first, with no day headers (each row shows its date). A Later post is in Unread too. Main feed's Later holds every source's, not only the main feed's.
- **List**, Show history (decision 034): the posts you opened or read (an `opened` or `reading_session` interaction; never a bulk mark read), each once, at its last open, newest first, under day headers by the day you opened it (Today, Yesterday, then dates, your time zone). The rows are the usual rows; the time column is when you opened it, and a post opened days after its date says `published <date>`. In every scope; the Main feed's History holds every source's, as Later does. The rail keeps it (a track or source opens on what you read there). It loads afresh each time you come back, so the post just read leads; `Shift+A` does not work in it.
- **List**, by-source view: one section per source, its name linking to that source's scope, up to 3 rows, then "N more from <source> →".
- **Foot:** an infinite-scroll sentinel, a visible "Load more" button, or "You've reached the end."
- The list column is `max-w-5xl`, left-aligned next to the rail.
- **Search page** (`/search`): the same rail, where each link narrows the search to that scope (the current one marked), and the toolbar titled `Search` with, below it, the field holding the words (20rem, full width on a phone), then chips beside it: the scope searched from, pressed with a ×, which pressing takes off (every post); `Later`, `Kept`, `News, last 30 days`, `Classics` (one at a time; issue 0056; decision 043 names News's range); `Last week`, `Last month`, `Last year` (none pressed is any time). Below, `Order newest / best match`. The results are list rows, newest first, with no day headers (each row shows its date) and the matching line in place of the excerpt, up to 2 lines, its words in weight 600 and `text` (never a fill); a row that matched only by its title or source shows its excerpt. 20 a page. A story shows once, at its best-matching post, with the feed's `▸ covered by …` line (decision 035), unless the search is of one source, Later or Kept. Under `News, last 30 days` the rows are the stories job's News of the last 30 days (a story whose posts match, with a post in the scope), each once with its covered-by line; empty: "No News stories match “xyz”". Under `Classics` the results are the Classics view's rows (title, cited by, Later, Hide), the classics whose title or address matches, up to 100; empty: "No classics match “xyz”" with "Search everything". Before any words: one `text-2` line saying what search reads and that word forms (English and Russian), "phrases" and -word work.

## States

- **normal:** unread rows under day headers.
- **empty:** nothing unread: "You're all caught up" + "Show all". Later: "Nothing in Later" ("Press l on a post, or its clock button, to read it later. Finishing it takes it out."). Kept: "No kept posts". Updated: "No updated pages yet". History: "Posts you open show up here". All, nothing at all: "No posts yet" + "Add a source". When ingestion has stopped, the unread empty state says "No new posts: ingestion has stopped" instead of "caught up" (P11).
- **empty-scope:** a track or source with no posts: "No posts here yet. Posts appear once ingestion collects them." + a quiet "This track/source in Sources" link (P7).
- **loading:** a six-row skeleton in the list column; rail lines read "Loading…".
- **error:** `ErrorState` "Could not load the feed" with Retry; a failed next page is an inline error `Notice` with Retry, keeping the loaded rows. Tracks that fail in the rail say "× Could not load tracks."
- **thread:** badlogicgames' X thread as one row, `▸ Thread · 7 posts` open: its five replies in their order, then a quote of its first post, all at one indent; above it steipete's repost, `steipete ↻ from @badlogicgames`, a story of 2 (badlogicgames, whom you follow, wrote it: `covered by 2 · steipete, badlogicgames`).
- **story:** three stories open as trees: GPT-6.1 Terra led by OpenAI's own post (Hacker News' row of its address, two citers; `Also in roundups: LessWrong`), Claude Fable 5 whose origin is Anthropic's page outside INSIGHT (this row, a forward under the post it copies, `↳ denissexy` at 390, and Simon; two roundups), and Ghostty leaving GitHub led by Mat Duggan's row (mitchellh.com's post read: @steipete's repost with `↻ reposted by @steipete`, Andrew Nesbitt under this row, `↳ matduggan.com` at 390).
- **worst:** long and unbroken titles, non-Latin and RTL text, missing titles ("Untitled post" when the API has no text to make one from), 1,000+ unread counts, huge source names (dev `?data=worst` / `huge`).
- **search:** results under the field and chips, the matched words marked. Loading is the skeleton ("Searching"), a failure "Could not search" with Retry.
- **search-empty:** "No posts match “xyz”" with the active chips (`In Main feed · Last week`) and the one inverted button "Search everything", which takes every chip off; with no chip on, "Try other words, or fewer of them." and no button.

## Interactions & keys

- `j`/`k` move the selection (loads more at the end), `o`/Enter open (marks read), `m` read, `l` read later, `s` keep (the star), `Shift+A` mark read from the top down to the selection (confirm dialog, then a toast with Undo for 5 s; not in Later or History, whose order is not the feed's), `?` the shortcut table with the single-key switch.
- Read, Later and Keep are optimistic; failures roll back with a toast.
- Opening or marking read a story's lead also reads its copies and shares (not the other reports, which stay unread and reachable from the row); the Main feed's and a track's unread counts count rows, so a story counts once.
- **Later never expires.** A post leaves it when finished: its end reached on the post page, or marked read (the circle, `m`). Opening it does not (even though opening marks it read), nor does `Shift+A` (catching up is not finishing), nor marking it unread again. `l` or the clock take it out by hand.
- New posts never reorder the loaded list; the pill reloads and scrolls to the top. The unread counts (pill and rail) are checked every 10 seconds while a fetch job runs and every 60 otherwise, never in a hidden tab, and a run saves each source as it is fetched, so the pill appears while posts arrive.
- Hovering a row reveals its actions instantly with a mouse; on touch and on the selected row they always show.
- Back from a post selects and scrolls to the post read last.
- On the search page `j`/`k`, `o`, `m`, `l` and `s` work on the results as on the feed; a result opens as a post on its own (no feed context), so the post page's `j`/`k` are off. `Shift+A` says it does not work in search results.
- Rail links keep `view` and a non-default `filter`.

## Worst cases

- Titles wrap to 2 lines and never ellipsise at 1 (`[overflow-wrap:anywhere]`, `line-clamp-2`); `dir="auto"` on titles and excerpts.
- Source names middle-truncate (56 chars; 26 in the rail) with the full name in `title`.
- Counts use `formatCount` (`1,284`) and never truncate; the scope title truncates, its count does not.
- A deleted source with posts has no rail row; its posts stay in the main feed.
- 320px: the toolbar's count moves under the title; actions are 44px.
- Below `sm` (decision 043): only the title row sticks (about 60px under the top bar, was 220 with Show, View and Posts); a story line's title has its own line.
- Below `sm` (decision 045) a row is a grid: the title across it (about 346px at 390, 316 at 360), then the meta lines and the excerpt beside the two actions (44px on touch, vertically centred), then the story's line and its open tree across the row. The time column is hidden and its time joins the first meta line, `Simon Willison · 11:09 · 1 min read` (`Oct 27` where the row has no day header). A repost's `↻ from @…`, a Hacker News item's `1,675 points · 1,628 comments` and a started post's length with its bar take a second line, a page's `published …` or `updated …` a third; no line starts on a dot. The source has no 12-character floor there (its line holds only the time and the length); a long name is cut before the length is, and beside a short one the length may cut rather than run under the actions. A story's line may take two lines, left-aligned, so News's and covered-by's names show (`▸ 8 sources (1 share) · in 1 roundup · Anthropic,` / `Simon Willison, +6`). From `sm` up the row is unchanged: the meta line keeps its order (source, repost, counts, length, bar, note) through `sm:order-*`.

## A11y & motion

- The stream is an ARIA `feed` of `article`s with `aria-posinset`/`aria-setsize`; day headers are `aria-hidden` and each row reads its day with its time. By-source groups are labelled `section`s.
- Roving tabindex: one row is a tab stop; selection is `aria-current`. Every icon button has a label and a title with its key.
- Results (read, "Saved for later" / "Removed from Later", "Kept" / "No longer kept", marked N posts) are announced in a polite live region.
- No motion on j/k, hover, filters or rail (P12). The pill and the drawer get one short ease-out; the drawer leaves the way it came.

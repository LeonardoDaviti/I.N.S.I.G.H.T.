# Reader

Code: `pages/ReaderPage.tsx`, `pages/SearchPage.tsx`, `components/reader/*` (`ReaderNavRail`, `FeedToolbar`, `SearchField`, `FeedList`, `FeedItem`, `FeedGroup`, `NewPostsPill`, `ShortcutHelp`), `lib/search.ts`.

## Purpose

The home of INSIGHT (P1): the posts to read, newest first, scoped to everything, one track or one source, and search, the way into every post already stored. It reads; it never fetches (P7).

## Entry & URL

- `/` is the Main feed: its unread posts from the sources shown in it. `/read/:scopeKind/:scopeId?` scopes it: `/read/folder/<id>` (a track), `/read/source/<id>` (a source). An unknown scope kind redirects to `/`.
- `/tracks/:id` redirects (replace) to `/read/folder/:id` (decision 010).
- Query: `?filter=unread|later|starred|all|history` (unread is the default and stays out of the URL; `starred` is Kept), `?view=stream|by_source|news|classics|topic` (News and Classics, decision 036, on the Main feed and a track; a source ignores them; Topic on a track only, while Mark X is on, issue 0145), `?all_sources=1` (a topic track's every post of its sources, v10.12.1; never carried to the next scope), dev-only `?data=worst|empty|one|huge`.
- Reached from the wordmark, the `feed` nav word, the rail, ⌘K (Main feed, By source, one entry per track) and Back from a post. ⌘K also reaches Later. Old links keep working: `/?filter=starred` opens the main feed with Show kept.
- `/search?q=…` is the one results view (issue 0040): `scope=all|folder:<id>|source:<id>` (none searches every post), `filter=later|starred|history|news|classics`, `days=7|30|365`, `order=newest|best` (none: by the words, issue 0139). New words push a history entry; a chip or the order replaces it. Reached from the toolbar's field (scoped to what is on screen: the scope, and Later or Kept when that is the list shown) and from ⌘K "Search posts…" (every post).

## States

- **normal:** unread rows under day headers.
- **empty:** nothing unread: "You're all caught up" + "Show all". Later: "Nothing in Later" ("Press l on a post, or its clock button, to read it later. Finishing it takes it out."). Kept: "No kept posts". Updated: "No updated pages yet". History: "Posts you open show up here". All, nothing at all: "No posts yet" + "Add a source". When ingestion has stopped, the unread empty state says "No new posts: ingestion has stopped" instead of "caught up" (P11).
- **empty-scope:** a track or source with no posts: "No posts here yet. Posts appear once ingestion collects them." + a quiet "This track/source in Sources" link (P7).
- **loading:** a six-row skeleton in the list column; rail lines read "Loading…".
- **error:** `ErrorState` "Could not load the feed" with Retry; a failed next page is an inline error `Notice` with Retry, keeping the loaded rows. Tracks that fail in the rail say "× Could not load tracks."
- **thread:** oddlogic_games' X thread as one row, `▸ Thread · 7 posts` open: its five replies in their order, then a quote of its first post, all at one indent; above it toolsmith_pete's repost, `toolsmith_pete ↻ from @oddlogic_games`, a story of 2 (oddlogic_games, whom you follow, wrote it: `covered by 2 · toolsmith_pete, oddlogic_games`).
- **story:** three stories open as trees: GPT-6.1 Terra led by OpenAI's own post (Hacker News' row of its address, two citers; `Also in roundups: LessWrong`), Claude Fable 5 whose origin is Anthropic's page outside INSIGHT (this row, a forward under the post it copies, `↳ dennis_it` at 390, and Simon; two roundups), and Ghostty leaving GitHub led by Mat Duggan's row (mitchellh.com's post read: @toolsmith_pete's repost with `↻ reposted by @toolsmith_pete`, Andrew Nesbitt under this row, `↳ matduggan.com` at 390).
- **worst:** long and unbroken titles, non-Latin and RTL text, missing titles ("Untitled post" when the API has no text to make one from), 1,000+ unread counts, huge source names (dev `?data=worst` / `huge`).
- **search:** results under the field and chips, the matched words marked. Loading is the skeleton ("Searching"), a failure "Could not search" with Retry.
- **search-history:** `History` pressed (issue 0139): only the posts you opened or read, last open first, each row's date its last open; no story folds.
- **search-markx:** Mark X on (issue 0139): up to 3 entity cards above the results, hairline rows (`border-line`), the name in weight 600, `type · by maker` in `text-2`, `N posts · N voices` in `text-3` at the right (wrapping under on a phone), each opening `/markx/entities/:id` (a merged entity shows the one it was merged into); a row only Mark X's English reading matched shows that headline as its line, its words marked, after a quiet `Mark X ·` in `text-3` (0.75rem), so you see why it matched.
- **search-empty:** "No posts match “xyz”" with the active chips (`In Main feed · Last week`) and the one inverted button "Search everything", which takes every chip off; with no chip on, "Try other words, or fewer of them." and no button.

## Interactions & keys

- `j`/`k` move the selection (loads more at the end), `o`/Enter open (marks read), `m` read, `l` read later, `s` keep (the star), `Shift+A` mark read from the top down to the selection (confirm dialog, then a toast with Undo for 5 s; not in Later or History, whose order is not the feed's), `?` the shortcut table with the single-key switch.
- Read, Later and Keep are optimistic; failures roll back with a toast.
- Opening or marking read a story's lead also reads its copies and shares (not the other reports, which stay unread and reachable from the row); the Main feed's and a track's unread counts count rows, so a story counts once.
- **Later never expires.** A post leaves it when finished: its end reached on the post page, or marked read (the circle, `m`). Opening it does not (even though opening marks it read), nor does `Shift+A` (catching up is not finishing), nor marking it unread again. `l` or the clock take it out by hand.
- New posts never reorder the loaded list; the pill reloads and scrolls to the top. The unread counts (pill and rail) are checked every 10 seconds while a fetch job runs and every 60 otherwise, never in a hidden tab, and a run saves each source as it is fetched, so the pill appears while posts arrive.
- Hovering a row reveals its actions instantly with a mouse; on touch and on the selected row they always show.
- Back from a post selects and scrolls to the post read last.
- On the search page `j`/`k`, `o`, `m`, `l` and `s` work on the results as on the feed; a result opens as a post on its own (no feed context), so the post page's `j`/`k` are off. `Shift+A` says it does not work in search results.
- Rail links keep `view` and a non-default `filter`.

## Worst cases

- Titles wrap to 2 lines and never ellipsise at 1 (`[overflow-wrap:anywhere]`, `line-clamp-2`); `dir="auto"` on titles and excerpts.
- Source names middle-truncate (56 chars; 26 in the rail) with the full name in `title`.
- Counts use `formatCount` (`1,284`) and never truncate; the scope title truncates, its count does not.
- A deleted source with posts has no rail row; its posts stay in the main feed.
- 320px: the toolbar's count moves under the title; actions are 44px.
- Below `sm` (decision 043): only the title row sticks (about 60px under the top bar, was 220 with Show, View and Posts); a story line's title has its own line.
- Below `sm` (decision 045) a row is a grid: the title across it (about 346px at 390, 316 at 360), then the meta lines and the excerpt beside the two actions (44px on touch, vertically centred), then the story's line and its open tree across the row. The time column is hidden and its time joins the first meta line, `Simon Willison · 11:09 · 1 min read` (`Oct 27` where the row has no day header). A repost's `↻ from @…`, a Hacker News item's `1,675 points · 1,628 comments` and a started post's length with its bar take a second line, a page's `published …` or `updated …` a third; no line starts on a dot. The source has no 12-character floor there (its line holds only the time and the length); a long name is cut before the length is, and beside a short one the length may cut rather than run under the actions. A story's line may take two lines, left-aligned, so News's and covered-by's names show (`▸ 8 sources (1 share) · in 1 roundup · Anthropic,` / `Simon Willison, +6`). From `sm` up the row is unchanged: the meta line keeps its order (source, repost, counts, length, bar, note) through `sm:order-*`.

## A11y & motion

- The stream is an ARIA `feed` of `article`s with `aria-posinset`/`aria-setsize`; day headers are `aria-hidden` and each row reads its day with its time. By-source groups are labelled `section`s.
- Roving tabindex: one row is a tab stop; selection is `aria-current`. Every icon button has a label and a title with its key.
- Results (read, "Saved for later" / "Removed from Later", "Kept" / "No longer kept", marked N posts) are announced in a polite live region.
- No motion on j/k, hover, filters or rail (P12). The pill and the drawer get one short ease-out; the drawer leaves the way it came.

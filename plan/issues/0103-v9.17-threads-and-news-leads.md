Status: **spec** 2026-11-05, from the v9.16 reader review (private: `plan/research/review-v9.16-reader.md` W1, W2; cases `thread-follows-quoted-tweet`, `news-lead-from-another-story`, `news-lead-skips-release-share`, `news-names-followed-first`). ## Wrong today (the lab, v9.16)
1. **A self-reply threads under the account's previous tweet, not the tweet it quotes** (`story_rules.threads`).
   - One account posted about one model at 15:19, about another model's launch at 16:21–16:22, then at 16:39 quoted its own 15:19 tweet. The 16:39 post is threaded under 16:22 (its previous tweet), joining the two subjects into one 0050 story.
   - The week's #2 News row (12 sources, the second model's launch) is then led by the 15:19 post, and all 33 of its posts' pages say "In News: <the other model's title>".
   - 55 of 91 thread-joined quote posts hang from another tweet than the one they quote; about 5 of the 44 whose quoted tweet is stored join two subjects.
   - Came in with 0076 (on the server since c8a99cb). 0076's real-data check read the re-lead as neutral (see 0104).
2. **Without an origin post, a row is led by its first *report*, skipping earlier shares of the release page** (`news_rules.lead`; 2 of 88 rows).
   - The month's #1 row (15 sources) is led by a later discussion post complaining about the release, while a share of the release's repository page came 7 hours earlier.
3. **Names in post order put Reddit authors before followed sources** (`story_rules.named_voices`; 2 of 32 rows with 3+ voices): "u/a, u/b in r/x, +13" while six followed sources are in the +13.

## Decided
- **Slice A (stories):** a reply or quote of one's own tweet hangs under the stored tweet it quotes or replies to. Timeline order (the previous tweet) only when that target isn't stored. Measure with 0075's diff: list every story that splits or joins, and read each one.
- **Slice B (News, after A merges):**
  - when no post is the row's origin, the first post that **shares a 0050 story's origin page** leads, before the first report; otherwise the rule is unchanged (origins always lead; a roundup never leads, 0083);
  - `named_voices` orders followed sources (by first post) before Reddit authors and copies; the count and the set are unchanged (0082: the names are the voices counted).

## Done when
- The four cases above pass; every passing case still passes.
- Slice A's diff: every story that moves is read, and none joins two subjects that weren't joined before.
- The real-data check passes for each slice; its diff lists only these changes.

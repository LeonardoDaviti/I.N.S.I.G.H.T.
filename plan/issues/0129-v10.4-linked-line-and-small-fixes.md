# 0129 — v10.4 (Stealth) slice A: the linked-pages line counts only a source's own pages, once; the flaky search test

Status: **spec** 2026-11-07, from 0124 B (not built there: another issue was changing `db/repo_linked.py`) and the v10.0.2 reader review's W2 (private: `plan/research/review-v10.0.2-reader.md`). **Constraints:**
- fix the class, never a site; no real names in committed files (details in `plan/research/0129-build/`, gitignored);

## Wrong
1. **The linked-pages line counts another writer's posts** (W2): one newsletter source's 18 linked pages include 3 of another writer's newsletter. The source stores one cross-post at that writer's publication, and `OwnPages` (`utils/linked_rules.py`) learns that publication's `/p/<slug>` shape from it: every other page there becomes the source's. Fetch them would store the other writer's posts as the source's.
2. **It counts one page twice, at two hosts:** a source read at its platform address and at its own domain has the same `/p/<slug>` linked at both; each is a page. And a page stored at one of its hosts is listed as not stored at the other.
3. **`test_search`'s `test_only_matching_posts_in_the_scope_and_dates_compete` fails now and then** (seen 5 times, "under load"). Its two title hits are meant to tie, but each scratch source's name in the search vector is its random address (`https://test.invalid/mark-ix/<hex>`). A hex starting like `3e4f…` is read by Postgres' parser as a number in scientific notation and a word (two lexemes, not one), so that source's posts are one lexeme longer and rank lower (`ts_rank`'s length normalisation): the tie breaks by the random address, not by date. Not load: 6.6% of random addresses split (20,000 drawn), and the test fails when the first source's does: about 1 run in 15.

## Decided
1. **A shape on a publication is learned only from 2+ of the source's posts there**, except on the publication its address is on (a blog whose one stored post is at its feed's host is its own; a cross-post can't be at the publication the source is read from). `OwnPages(sources, address_publications=)`; `db/repo_linked` passes each source's address's publication (`SourceFacts.handle_host`).
2. **One page at two of a source's publications is one page:** `OwnPages.same_page(url)` keys an owned page by (its source, its path without a trailing slash, its query). In `linked_not_stored`:
   - links to its forms are one page, counted by its distinct linking posts, at the address on the publication holding more of the source's stored posts (then the one its address is on, then the first by address);
   - a stored post of the source at any of its forms makes the page stored.
3. **The test's sources get a fixed name** (`_source`'s default display name), so their vectors are of one length and the tie is the date's, as the test says. The search code is right (a longer name does rank lower); the test's random name was the bug. A regression test runs the tie with addresses that split.

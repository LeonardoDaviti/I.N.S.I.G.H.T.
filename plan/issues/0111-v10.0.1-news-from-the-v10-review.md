## Wrong today
1. **A row is titled by a story that joined it only by a name.**
   - The week's top row (20 sources) is about a paper, but it reads as an unrelated benchmark thread's first tweet. That thread joined because it mentions a model name in a comparison.
   - The paper's own story has no first post of its own, so `news_rules.lead` took the joined story's.
   - 6 of 128 leads come from a smaller story than the row's main one, and 2 of 128 rows are titled by another subject.
2. **A date joins rows as a name** ("nov 2026"), in 1 of 128 rows. Three more rows join by a generic term (a cloud, a technique, a number format), but telling those apart is Mark X.
3. **One owner counts as two voices** in 7 of 128 rows (a person's video channel and blog; a lab's two feeds), and 3 rows are News only because of it.
4. **The 30-day edge splits a row:** when its first post leaves the window, the row comes back led by its second post. The case `source-role-started-not-first` fails on that clock.
5. **Smaller:**
   - Mark X's first decision run waits up to an hour after a deploy;
   - the Mark X post panel reads "A story of 2 posts · 2 posts";
   - one Discover archive row names a path from another host;
   - a platform subdomain is listed among a company's own places;
   - licence pages (CC BY, Apache) are Classics.

## Decided
1. **A story that joined a row only by a name never supplies the row's lead.** The lead comes from the row's main story (by voices). Read every changed lead.
2. **A date or a month-year is no name** for News joining (0083's name rules).
3. **News voices are counted by owner,** with the same-owner rule Discover uses (0080/0109: a followed source's channels). The names list names the owner once.
4. **A row leaves the 30-day window whole** (by its first post's date), never re-led by its second post. Mark X's News compares against Mark IX on the same clock.
5. **Smaller:**
   - Mark X's first decision run fires when a start's story job finishes;
   - the panel's wording is fixed;
   - an archive row's path comes from its own host;
   - platform subdomains (podcasts., music., …) aren't a company's own places;
   - licence and legal pages are references, not Classics (address shapes, measured first).

**3. One owner, one voice** (`1226afa`)
- **The rule** (`discover_rules.owner_pairs`, `channel_owners`) is 0109's same-owner rule, between two followed sources. They are one owner when one of these holds:
  - a name of one is in the other's own place (its account, its site's word, a writer's label on a host of many writers), either way, **and** one links the other's posts or place;
  - or it is one place followed twice.
- **Two exceptions** (0109's two wrong pairs):
  - a platform's word (github, medium, youtube…) names no owner;
  - a source at a platform's own host (a code host's trending feed) has no place of its own.
- **A Reddit poster** whose name is a followed X, Telegram or YouTube account's handle is that account.
- **Where it is read:**
  - `SourceFacts.owner` and `owners`: the candidate pairs come from names, then one read of their links decides (16k links of 52 sources on the server's copy). `SourceFacts` takes 39 → 120 ms there.
  - `_story_post` sets `StoryPost.owner` and `other_owners`. So `news_rules.news` (the count and the tally) and Mark X's rows (made by `NewsService.rows` over `_story_post`) count alike.
  - `news_posts.voice` is unchanged. The owners the job counted by are kept in `system_settings` (`news_owners`). News and search's News filter name the voices by them (`story_voices`): one name per owner, a followed channel's name before a Reddit post's.
  - A source's facts print holds its owner, so a new owner queues its posts.
- **Measured, the owners:**
  - Server's copy: 19 owners of 2+ channels, each read right by its names and links (for example a podcaster's channel and blog, a lab's two feeds, a researcher's six places). 5 of them are one channel followed twice, and 2 are one site's two pages. Plus the one Reddit poster.
  - Lab: 20 owners, one debatable (a company's research news and its developers blog).
- **Measured, News** (head's full job on a copy of main's state, same clock):
  - Server's copy: 128 → 124 rows.
    - Gone (3, right): two rows of the podcaster's channel and blog ("2 sources · <channel>, <blog>"), and one of a lab's two feeds.
    - A count one lower (4, right): a release row (16 → 15: the maker's X account and his Reddit name), a model launch (11 → 10: a hub's blog and its video channel), and two essays' rows (3 → 2 each).

## Why (the sources review, 2026-11-04)
- One disabled account holds 559 first reports, the most of any source. Whether it was noise or signal can't be read from INSIGHT today.
- One large subreddit gives 1,372 posts in 30 days, of which you read 47.

## What
On each source's page and as columns in Sources, over 30 and 90 days:
- **first reports:** stories it broke (0064's tree);
- **repeats:** its posts in stories someone else broke, and its copies (same text, 0050);
- **what its posts are:** reports, commentary, roundups, tools, other (Shadow's readings);
- **off-topic share:** posts whose entities match none of your tracks' entities (0090);
- **a one-line verdict:**
  - "breaks news";
  - "good commentary";
  - "mostly repeats what N of your sources already gave you";
  - "mostly roundups";
  - "mostly off-topic".

The same numbers rank Discover's candidates once they've been followed for a while (0101).

## Measure
Compare the verdicts with your own keep / quiet choices on 30 sources.

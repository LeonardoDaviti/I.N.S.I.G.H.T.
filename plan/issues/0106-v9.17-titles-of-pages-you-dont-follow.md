## Wrong today (the lab, v9.16; measured read-only, 2026-11-05)
1. **Discover titles one page three ways in one answer** (W4).
   - An article line is titled by `_title_articles` (the stored post, else the link words, else the page read once). An origin line ("Started N of your stories") and an archive line are titled by `_story(root)`, the story's own origin title, and never read.
   - Of 137 origin lines over all 1,082 suggestions, **53 are untitled; 11 of them are titled in the same card's articles** (the #1 suggestion shows "site · section" for the story it started while its articles list the same address with its headline).
   - The archive rows' lines ("sources you follow that miss where your stories start"): **8 of 11 untitled**, and `discover_reads.work` reads only articles, so they never get one.
   - On the first page: 42 of 90 article lines and 27 of 64 origin lines untitled.
2. **Classics' titles** (W5): 182 classics, 162 of them pages no source of yours holds.
   - **56 are untitled** (the reader shows the address), and **21 are titled by a citation's words** ("<Author> et al., 2024").
   - A classic is titled by the most common link words of its citers (`classic_rules.classics`), not by the evidence a story's outside origin and Discover's lines are titled by (`origin_title`: the page's own words as a post quotes them, link words 2+ voices agree on, a link post's title…).
   - 0080's page-title read covers only Discover's first page; Classics are never read.
3. **One paper is two classics** (W5): its abstract and its PDF are two addresses (`/abs/<id>` with 8 voices and no title; `/pdf/<id>` with 3 voices, titled by a chart's caption). 2 of the 53 papers in Classics are split so.
   - On the lab's kept links, **7,763 addresses hold a paper id (6,668 papers), and 734 papers sit at 2+ addresses on one host**: the abstract, the PDF, the HTML, a version, a tracking query; publishers' DOIs under `/doi/`, `/doi/abs/`, `/doi/full/`, `/doi/epdf/`.
   - `page_aliases` (0070) makes another path of a page one page only for a slug of words with a stored page; a paper id is no slug.

## Decided
1. **One title per page, everywhere a page you don't follow is listed** (0080 decision 2, made one function, `services/page_titles.py`):
   1. the stored post's title;
   2. the evidence of every post linking it (`origin_title`, never the slug: the page's quoted words, then link words 2+ voices agree on, …); a tweet only by its own words;
   3. the page's own title, read once (stored with its date in `discover_reads`, 30 days);
   4. else no title (Discover: "site · section"; Classics: the address).
   - Discover builds **one url → title map per answer** and titles articles, origin lines and archive lines by it.
   - Classics' job titles a classic so (2), and the API reads (1) and (3) over it, for Classics and a post's background.
   - The read never overrides what the stored data says: it fills only a page with no title. A citation's words stay a title until Mark X tells one from a title (the review's W5 note); evidence alone changes 5 of the 21.
2. **The reads** (`discover_reads.work`, where Discover's reads run: an open of the first page, on a machine that reads):
   - the untitled article lines **and origin lines** of the first page's suggestions, and the untitled lines of every archive row;
   - the untitled classics (no stored post, no evidence title), at most once in 30 days each;
   - tweets are never read.
3. **The kept list stays kept** (0080 cold open): a read batch that stored anything changes `discover_inputs`, so at its end the list is made and kept **once** (`discover_list`), and the next open, in any process, reads one row.
4. **A paper id is one page on its host, whatever its path** (`addresses.paper_id`, 0080's paper-source shape):
   - a preprint's `yymm.nnnnn` as the address's last segment, its version and `.pdf` aside; a DOI (`10.<registrant>/<suffix>`) to the end of the path in the first three segments, case aside, a version and a file ending aside;
   - it is a shape rule: no host is named, and it holds on every host with such addresses (preprint servers, a paper hub's mirrors, publishers' DOI paths);
   - one address names the paper: the stored page's, else its page before its file (`pdf`, `epdf`, `.pdf`), then one without a query, then the shortest;
   - through `page_aliases`: a story's link to any path of the paper is a link to that one page (`_page_key` keys a paper by its id, so the incremental job reads every post linking any of its paths); Classics count its citers once over all its paths (an incremental batch reads every path of a paper it touches).
   - This changes the rules' stamp (a sample case is added): the first start regroups every story, as 0103 A did.

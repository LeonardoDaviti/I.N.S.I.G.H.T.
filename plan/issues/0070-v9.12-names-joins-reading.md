## A. Stories and News (backend rules)
1. **A name's history is counted in sources, not posts** (R8).
   - Today `NAME_MAX_USUAL = 1` counts posts in the 30 days before. pankajkumar_dev's 11 leak posts made "Gemini 4 Carbon" old, so the 28 Oct launch never surged.
   - Decided: count the distinct voices that said the name in those 30 days.
   - Measure first: re-measure 0051's precision table (never seen ~88% real, once ~75%, twice+ ~27%) in voices, with 30 names checked by hand.
   - If a channel's own repeated series name ("not much happened today") starts surging falsely, add the guard: the 48 h burst must hold more voices than the 30 days before.
   - Expected: ai_newz, turboproject, data_secrets' second post, r/LocalLLaMA and pankajkumar_dev join the Gemini 4 Carbon News story (case `gemini-4-carbon-leak`).
2. **A known name's later wave is judged too** (R8). When 3+ voices that didn't say a name before say it within 48 h, that burst is judged like a first one.
   - Expected: Mistral Large 4's 29 Oct open-weights posts are one News story (`mistral-large-4-open-weights`). Record DeepSeek V4.1 Flash and Gemini 4 Argon.
3. **Styled letters are plain letters when compared** (R8). Titles and search text are NFKC-normalized (Python's `unicodedata`) before name matching, title matching and the search index (stored text and query alike). Displayed text is unchanged.
   - Expected: akhaliq's "Introducing 𝙻𝙵𝙼𝟹" joins the LFM3 story.
   - List every lab title NFKC changes, and check none changes meaning.
4. **A publication's links to another publication on its site are citations** (0069 C Left open).
   - Links between subdomains of one site are dropped today as "the source's own site", so 28 Substack writer-to-writer links make no citation.
   - Decided: "own site" means the post's own publication (0069's `publication_of`), as voices do.
   - Measure for menus and footers: the boilerplate rule (0050) still drops links repeated across a source's posts.
5. **One source, one title, one body, two addresses is one post** (R10).
   - Within one source, the same title and the same body (same hash or opening, or one side empty) within a day is one post. The newer address wins, with reading state carried over, as 0038 did.
   - The normalizer drops a trailing `/` inside a query value.
   - Expected: about 56 pairs in 11 sources. Xeiaso's "No way to prevent this" and Smol's "not much happened today" stay apart (their bodies differ).
   - Migration **0052**.
6. **A post whose first link is the story's origin is a member, not a mention** (R11). Case `reddit-release-is-not-roundup` (r/LocalLLaMA's "mistralai/Mistral-Large-4-1T-A52B · Hugging Face").
7. **One page under two paths of its own site joins by address** (R12). On one host, the same last path segment of 3+ words (a slug, not an id), with a language segment skipped as 0067 reads it, is one page.
   - Expected: Anthropic's "Redesigning source control" under `/research/` and `/engineering/` (6 posts); 11 pages, 25 posts.
   - Measure for false joins (`/blog/x` vs `/docs/x`).
8. **A Saved page is never a voice** (R17). It can still be the origin node, the page everyone links, shown as the page, never "first".
9. **A thread joins a story only from the post that links or quotes it, and the posts after it** (R20). Its earlier posts stay apart. Expected: pankajkumar_dev's "Fable 5.5 coming this week" leaves the Anthropic S-1 tree.

## B. Rows, dates and labels (reader)
1. **Undated posts don't flood the feed** (R7).
   - The full-text read takes `page_date` when the feed gives none.
   - Undated items of one fetch keep their feed order below the source's dated posts, not all at the fetch moment.
   - Expected: Google Developers Blog's 20 rows don't lead the Main feed.
2. **A date alone shows as a date** (R14). The reader gets 0064's `trusted_time`: no invented "04:00", and the day is placed in UTC. There are 3,304 such posts.
3. **Repost and thread lines name the right person once** (R13):
   - a repost's tree line names the author: `@danintheory · … · ↻ sama`;
   - the meta line reads `badlogicgames ↻ from @thsottiaux`, without repeating the source;
   - a thread reply's quote of its own thread shows `↩ quotes the post above`, not the whole card;
   - the post page doesn't list the replies it already read above.
4. **Hacker News rows read like rows** (R18). The four hnrss lines leave the body, and the meta line reads `1,675 points · 1,628 comments`. There are 17 of 62 such posts.
5. **A title taken from the body isn't read twice** (R20). The body starts after it: 3,039 Telegram, 680 X and 303 RSS posts.
6. **0069 B's Left open titles:**
   - `http://agentville.world` becomes "> accessible…";
   - a trailing custom emoji is lost;
   - a Telegram link preview runs site, title and description together;
   - titles are cut at an abbreviation (`U.S.`);
   - an album's caption on its last item isn't folded.

   Measure each on the lab and fix the class, not the example.
7. **Phones** (R9): below `sm`, only the title bar sticks; Show, View and Posts scroll away. The source name keeps 12+ characters, and `published …` and `↻ …` move to a second meta line. In covered-by lines, the title takes its own line.
8. **The News search chip** says "last 30 days" (R19).

## C. Titles everywhere, and one stuck job
1. **Discover and origins use one title rule** (R15).
   - Discover's article titles use 0068's `origin_title`, not the most common link words: "Video" ×42, "tweet" ×23.
   - An outside tweet is titled by its text in a stored quote's `<blockquote>`; 51 of 54 tweet origins have one.
   - An outside page linked by a stored post that is only that link (a Just Links or Reddit link post) takes that post's title.
   - A slug's leading number is dropped.
2. **A fetch job orphaned by a restart is closed** (R20). At start, a job still `running` from before the start becomes `interrupted`, so the top bar never says "fetching · 1 of 1" forever. Check the code path first; record whether the server can hit it.

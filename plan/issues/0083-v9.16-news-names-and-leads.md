# 0083 — v9.16 News: a name said inside a joined word, and a roundup never leads

## Why (the lab, v9.15)
1. **A product name written as one word never matches its words.**
   - A repo's name ("org/Model-V4.1-Flash-1017 · Weights") is one word to the name rule (`_lower` reads its hyphens and dots as spaces, inside the word). `phrases` makes runs of words, so the title never says "flash 1017", and the post is never in that name's burst.
   - One release made three News rows (one joined by "flash 1017", one of its repo post, one of a quantised copy's repo post).
   - Over the last 30 days: 42 (post, name) pairs in 46 surges where the name is said only inside a joined word. Some are joined anyway by their links.
2. **A weekly digest leads a News row two days after the event (1 row).**
   - The digest joins its 0050 story as a report by its first link, and is also the story's roundup (`story_mentions`).
   - The origin is an outside page, so `news_rules.lead` takes the row's first report: the digest, over two earlier shares.

## Decided
1. **A word its separators join is read as its parts too** (`news_rules.phrases`).
   - The runs of 1 to 3 words are made from the title's words and from their parts, so "org/Model-V4.1-Flash-1017" says "flash 1017" and "model v4.1".
   - What *names* a post (`names`) is unchanged: a joined word never starts a surge on its own; it joins one's burst and counts as said in the 30 days before.
   - General, no lists of sites or names.
2. **A roundup never leads a row that has a report** (`news_rules.lead`). Measured first (below), and the rule as first written is wrong:
   - "A post with `ROUNDUP_ANCHORS`+ anchors" is no roundup test for leads. Even with the origin kept first, it changes 9 leads on main's rows: 3 right, 6 a first-hand post with many links (an article, a maker's own post) replaced by a later share or retelling. Passing over an origin too, it changes 19 on the head's rows; the extra ones are a lab's own post replaced by a retelling a day later.
   - What separates them: the row's **origin always leads** (as today); only when there is none, the first report or share **the stories list as a roundup** (`story_mentions`) is passed over. On main's rows that changes 2 leads, both right: the weekly digest (W3) and a vendor's "new models, agent tools and games" roundup, each now led by the post about the release.
   - Built after 0076 merged (branch `issue-0083-leads-and-news-link`): one argument to `lead` (`roundups`), one column in `repo_news.news_posts` (`roundup`: the post is in `story_mentions`), one line in `news_service.news_on`.
3. **A post's News row on its page** (from 0082's "Next in v9.16", third bullet; 77 of 333 lines of the v9.15 review: a News lead's post page shows nothing of its row).
   - The post detail answer carries `news`: the News row the post is in (the last `NEWS_DAYS`), `{id, title, voices, lead_id, scope}`, as the Main feed's News shows it (its title is the row's, the lead's); a row only on a source outside the Main feed, as that source's News. `null` when the post is in none.
   - The post page says, quietly, above "Builds on": **"In News: <row title> · N sources"**, the title linking the News it is in (`/?view=news`, or `/read/source/<id>?view=news`). The News view has no way to open at one row; it is a short ranked list, so the link opens it, not the row. A lead's page linking the lead's own page would say nothing.
   - On the post page only (its chunk); nothing in the feed's.

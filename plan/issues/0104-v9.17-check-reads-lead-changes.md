# 0104 — v9.17: the real-data check reads News lead changes

Status: **built, not merged** 2026-11-05 (branch `issue-0104-check-leads`); **spec** 2026-11-05, from the v9.16 reader review (private review W1: 0076's check saw a News row change its lead to a post about another subject and called it neutral).

**Constraints:** download nothing; no real names in committed files; tooling only (no reader behaviour changes).

## Wrong today
- `scripts/realdata-check.sh` (0075) diffs News rows but treats a changed lead title as neutral. A lead that moves to a post that doesn't name the row's subject, or that isn't the row's first post, is exactly what a reader notices first.

## Decided
- In the News diff, a lead change is **flagged** (listed under "needs reading", and counted) when the new lead's title doesn't contain the row's name (0083's name), or the new lead isn't the row's origin or first post while the old one was.
- Flagged changes don't fail the check on their own; they are listed with both titles, the row's name and its size, so the reader of the report judges them. Cases still decide PASS/FAIL.
- Replay: run the check for 0076's merge (`--base` its parent, `--head` its merge) and show that the Kimi-style re-lead is flagged.

## Done when
- The check's tests cover flagged and unflagged lead changes.
- The replay of 0076's merge flags that re-lead, and a check of main against itself flags nothing.

## Built
Branch `issue-0104-check-leads` (2026-11-05), on main 43a8804. Not merged. Tooling only: `backend/insight_core/checks/` and its tests; no reader code, no migration.
- **A re-led row is one row.** The News diff keyed rows by their lead, so a row whose lead moved to another of its posts read as "gone row" + "new row" (0076's report: the launch row so). Now rows not matched by lead are paired by their posts (they share at least half the smaller row's posts, most shared first, each once) and listed as one **lead change**, with both titles; its posts, voices and first changes are listed under the old lead's id.
- **Flagged for reading** (`lead changed: needs reading`, `diff.NEEDS_READING`), as Decided:
  - the new lead's shown title doesn't say the row's name: the name most of the row's posts joined by (`news_posts.name`, 0083's), read by the head version's own `news_rules.phrases`; or
  - the old lead was the row's origin (a post starting its 0050 story, `parent_reason IS NULL`) or its first post (`news_posts.position` 0) and the new one is neither.
  - Each is listed in full (never sampled), with both titles, the row's name, its size, the reason and the new lead's id. The count sits under the verdict ("**Needs reading: N**") and above the diff's table; it never fails the check. Cases still decide PASS/FAIL.

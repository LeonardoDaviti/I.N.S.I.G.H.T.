# 0269 — v10.31 hotfix: what the reader reads (track page placement, entity briefs, issue points)

Status: **built** (Stealth v10.31.1), not deployed. No migration. Cases (plan/research/lab/cases.json):
`track-page-stale-after-worker-change`, `entity-brief-from-one-post`, `issue-points-cite-rows-left-the-week`.

## 1. The kept track page follows its kept placement (markx/tracks.page_inputs)

Production (2026-11-21, GET only, 3D track): /why (live) said `discover` for the solar tracker and LastGraph; the
track page listed them `joined`. Watched every minute 12:16-12:19 UTC: the page said Discover 418 / joined 34 while
the sidebar's counts (the same kept placement, `track_kept.counts`) said Discover 451, and live /why counted 439;
at 12:19 the page moved to 439 / 13 (the 0169 one-run joins gone) together with the sidebar.

Cause: since 0264 a kept track's page reads its kept placement, and when it was placed (`placed_at`) was only in the
page's *loose* part. The API sends kept bytes while the strict stamp is the same and before their KEPT_LAG
(15 minutes) ends; only the jobs process's "tracks" view rebuilt a page whose loose part moved. A refresh run by the
ingestion (ports.keep_derived) or after a plan moved the placement, and the page kept showing the older one until
the jobs view's next build or the lag's end. (Cuboid was re-checked by the worker during the watch: both runs in,
joined again: correct.)

Fix: `placed_at` is in the strict stamp too: a page kept from an older placement is never sent; the next open after
any refresh builds it (the jobs view places and then builds in one look, so it is usually kept already). Staleness
for the worker's changes is now the placement's own: at most REFRESH_GAP + the refresh's pace.
Test: test_markx_tracks.KeptPlaces.test_the_kept_page_follows_a_placement_of_the_workers_change (fails without the
fix: 'joined' != 'discover').

Seen, not changed (left to the performance work): the track page GET itself is 0.03-0.06 s kept; the reading views
of a track (`GET /api/feed?scope=folder:<track>`) took 1.3-1.4 s for each of three tracks, because
track_feed.track_posts places the track live (tracks.placed over every judged item: 52k on the 3D track) on every
request instead of reading the kept placement; /tracks/counts 0.29 s, /tracks/suggestions 0.42 s,
/api/feed/unread-counts 0.5 s.

## 2. Entity briefs (markx/entity_brief.py, frontend EntityBrief)

- Shown only when SHOW_POSTS (5) posts were read (a things question of the entity answered) and a `what_it_is` line
  is about the entity itself. The schema (version 2: every briefed entity's posts are read again, within
  ASKS_A_DAY) gives what_it_is a `subject` field: the name the definition is about as the post writes it; a line
  counts as the entity's when its subject is one of its members' names (folded). "<Entity> Camera is a camera app"
  is that product's line, not the entity's. Rows asked under schema 1 have no subject: no brief until read again.
- Planned with MIN_POSTS 5 subject/actor posts (was 3).
- Posts read: the entity's own and its maker's posts first (person_page.by_sources: the sources that are the entity
  or its maker, via its voice or the owner's yes), then the newest; MAX_POSTS 12.
- Display: each line is `text` plus a `note` shown apart (never glued): what_it_is: the definition, note stage ·
  maker; how_it_works: the step, note its detail (unless already in it); a result: "what → result" ("what" left out
  when it is the entity's own name), note the evidence only when it gives figures the line does not. Lines of one
  kind with the same figures and the same key words (folded words of 4+ letters) are one line citing every post;
  anything less alike is left to the joins. Haiku's own words inside a value (e.g. a result reading "the post's
  opinion: ...") are not rewritten. Entity page shape 4 (`note`).
- Selection: opens ARE recorded for entity pages (GET /api/markx/entities/{id} -> kept_pages.opened 'entity'; the UI
  reads that route), so `candidates` stays the 50 most opened in 14 days. Why Code Mode has no brief needs the
  server's rows (mx_page_opens for its id; its subject/actor post count; ASKS_A_DAY spent by entities opened more);
  not checked (no DB access, and GET-ing its page would count an open).

Tests: test_entity_brief_0261 (the 5-post gate, the sub-product definition, merged lines, what → result).
Frontend: entityBrief.test.tsx.

## 3. A week's points cite only the week's rows (markx/track_brief.py)

- `points(..., within=)`: a week's issue shows a point only when every row it cites is in its sections (the shown
  things and the practices folded into them). Production: the Security & Privacy week of 2026-11-16 showed points
  asked at 10:07 about old Mac malware rows that re-dating moved out of the week.
- `plan`: the week in progress is asked again within BRIEF_EVERY when most (over half) of the rows its newest brief
  cites are no longer its rows (`_cites_left`); otherwise at most every BRIEF_EVERY, as before; never while one waits.
Test: test_track_briefer_0245.Flow.test_points_about_rows_that_left_the_week_are_not_shown_and_the_week_is_asked_again.

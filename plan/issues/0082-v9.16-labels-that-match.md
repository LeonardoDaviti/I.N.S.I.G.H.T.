# 0082 — v9.16 first slice: a story's count names what it counts, and "Started it" only when it was first

Status: **spec** (2026-11-04), from the v9.15 reader review (private: `plan/research/review-v9.15-reader.md`, cases `news-voices-named`, `source-role-started-not-first`). The first slice of v9.16, before 0076. Small; merges on its own (the merge rule, 0075).

**Constraints:** download nothing; no real names in committed files or fixtures; nothing merges unless `scripts/realdata-check.sh` passes.

## Wrong today (the lab, v9.15)
1. **A count that doesn't match its names (9 of 93 News rows).**
   - A row reads "10 sources · A, B, +8" but opens to 4 names: each Reddit author is a voice (0050: on Reddit the person who posted), while the names list each followed source once.
   - The reverse also happens: two feeds of one publication posting the same text are one voice, but both are named ("says 8, names 9").
   - The same mismatch is in the feed row's "covered by N" and the post page's "Covered by N" wherever a story has Reddit authors or same-publication copies.
2. **"Started it" when it wasn't first (4 of 43 origin roles).**
   - 0077 K marks the source whose post sits at the story's origin link, not the source that posted first.
   - On an aggregator (an HN post of an outside page), the post at the origin is just the first share of a page someone else published. So the source page says "Started it" while the post page (0072 D) says another post came first.

## Decided
1. **One number, and the names are the same set** (keeps decision 042: one number for a story everywhere, its voices).
   - The names list is built from the voices the count counts, one name per voice: a Reddit author is named `u/name` (with its subreddit when space allows), and a publication's copies are named once, by the publication.
   - "+N" is the voices not named.
   - The rule is one function, used by the feed row, the post page, News and a source's News.
   - It doesn't touch Discover, whose "your sources" wording keeps 0067's rule (no Reddit users among "your sources"); 0080 changes Discover.
2. **"Started it" means first.**
   - A source's role is `origin` only when its post is the story's first in time by the rules the post page uses (0072 D: `story.first`, a date alone as early as its day's first timed post).
   - Otherwise it gets its strongest other role.
   - When nobody followed posted first (the origin is an outside page and the first post is a share), the first sharer is "Shared", not "Started it".

## Done when
- The cases `news-voices-named` and `source-role-started-not-first` pass. Every passing case still passes.
- On the lab: for every story, the count equals the names plus "+N", and every "Started it" agrees with the post page's first.
- The real-data check passes, and its diff lists only these labels.

# 0096 — Weekly "what changed": a digest for learning, not a feed

Status: **future** (2026-11-04). Mark X, after 0090, 0091 and 0095.

## What
Once a week, about ten lines, each cited:
- **new entities** that appeared in your sources (a new lab, a new model family);
- **surges:** entities whose main-subject posts rose against their own baseline, per exact version (0090 E);
- **follow-ups** to stories you read (0091);
- **numbers that moved:** prices, scores, funding (0095);
- **connections** that formed between things you follow.

## How
- Haiku writes it from those lists, the way the daily briefing is written (F12: preferred on 15 of 15 days, no unsupported line).
- Every figure and version is checked against its posts in code.

## Measure
- Your preference against a plain "top stories of the week" list, on 4 weeks.
- Unsupported lines must be 0.

# 0100 — Study pages: one question or topic, everything needed to understand it on one page

Status: **future** (2026-11-04). Mark X, complex; composes 0091, 0097, 0098 and 0090.

## What
Pick a question or a topic ("open-weight models this year", "AI chip export rules"). One page shows:
- **its timeline:** events and numbers in order (0091, 0095);
- **the chain:** what led to what;
- **the entities involved,** and how they relate;
- **where sources disagree** (0097);
- **the primary sources:** first reports, papers, official pages, classics;
- **open questions,** answered from your library as far as it can (0098).

The page updates as posts arrive and marks what's new since you last studied it (0094).

## Measure
Your review of 5 study pages on topics you know well: correct, complete, worth the time.

# 0094 — Since you last looked: what's new on a story, entity or track since you opened it

Status: **future** (2026-11-04). Mark X, after 0090 (entities) and 0091 (connections).

## What
- **On a story:** new members, follow-ups and reactions since you last opened it.
- **On an entity:** new events, versions and numbers since your last visit (0090 F, 0095).
- **On a track:** the stories and connections that formed since you last read it.
- **A small "new since you looked" marker** on rows you've opened before.

## How
- The interaction log (0052) gives the last time you opened each story or entity.
- The graph's validity times (0091) give what appeared after it.
- No model is needed.

## Done when
On fixtures, a story opened yesterday shows exactly the members, follow-ups and connections added since.

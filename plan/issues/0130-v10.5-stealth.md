## Why
- **The entity queue is easy for the owner, so the bar is too strict.** 509 open on the server, every one a Haiku
  `same`, held back by a guard: below 0.9 (309), the version guard (127), collision (47, never automatic: 55% right),
  the fields parser (13), maker unstated (10), chain / extra word (3). The owner's 51 answers: 48 same, 2 another
  version, 1 different (94% agree with Haiku). Only ≥ 0.9 was ever measured (97.6%, 40/41).
- **The archive run broke on four kinds of source** (2026-11-07, run from the owner's server):
  - every newsletter-platform archive (8 of 8) fails: the platform's archive API answers HTTP 400 "offset: Invalid
    value" to `offset=0` — the API changed;
  - one site reader (a weekly AI newsletter) reads 38 pages and finds 0 posts — its page layout changed;
  - one lab's blog feed answers 404 — the feed moved;
  - three page-watched or feed sources finish "success" with 0 new posts while the site lists more (a research
    org: 102 re-read, 153 unreached; two lab news pages: 10 and 3 unreached).
- **Telegram and X archives have no horizon.** An archive runs to the source's start (0015); the Telegram core back to
  1 Nov 2025 is about 2.4k posts, its full history about 60k. 0102's "back to a date" was a draft.
- **Translation exists only as a task kind.** `translate` (guide translate-v1, 0086 F5: meaning kept 87%, misleading
  2%) has a schema, checks and the worker, but nothing plans it, no button asks for it and nothing shows it.

## What it does

### A. The entity queue, measured (identity.py, entity_stage.py, the Entities tab)
1. **Bands, measured on labels.** Split the owner's queue answers (and ~100 more the owner labels; the queue shows
   the band) by confidence band (≥ 0.85, 0.80–0.85, < 0.80) and by guard. A band whose Wilson lower bound is ≥ 0.90
   and point ≥ 0.97 (today's automatic bar) becomes automatic, per band and guard, as a config row, not code.
2. **A second opinion.** A queued `same` is asked again with two example posts per name (a new guide `alias-v2`, or
   alias-v1 with the examples; measure which). Two `same` ≥ 0.85 agreeing: automatic, with its redirect (undoable).
   A disagreement stays in the queue, marked. Collisions keep their rule: asked 2–3 times, only ever proposed.
3. **The version guard narrowed.** It holds a pair only when both names carry versions and they differ (GPT-6 vs 6.1),
   or one carries a model version the other's family has several of. A year or edition on one side only ("DevDay"
   vs "<Lab> DevDay 2026"), or the maker on one side only, goes to the second opinion instead.
4. **Measured before adopting:** on the owner's labels and the lab's unseen entity set; report right / wrong /
   missed with exact rows (private). Automatic merges must stay ≥ 97% right.

### B. Archives that work again (ingestion kinds, archive service)
1. **The newsletter platform's archive API:** find the current paging (offset ≥ 1, a cursor, or the sitemap) from
   the platform's own published behaviour on the server's logs; fall back to the publication's sitemap (0028's
   reader) when the API refuses. A fixture of the new answers; no fetch from the lab.
2. **The weekly newsletter's site reader:** its listing's new layout (fixture from the server's stored page, if any;
   else the reader falls back to the sitemap).
3. **A moved feed:** an archive or fetch that gets 404 on the feed runs the feed finder (0108: follow redirects, find
   the feed it moved to) and offers the repair on the source's sheet, per the source-adding rule.
4. **"Success, 0 new" while the site lists more:** the job says why (the listing ends, the pages are already stored
   under other addresses, or the reader can't reach them) and, for page-watched sources, reads the sitemap for the
   rest. Measured on the three sources.
5. **The runner's lesson:** a job interrupted by a deploy is `interrupted` and resumes from its checkpoint (it does);
   the Sources page offers "Resume interrupted archives" in one tap.

### C. Archives back to a date (0102 A, for Telegram and X)
- `ArchiveRequest.until` (a date): the job stops when its pages pass the date; the source sheet's archive section
  offers "back to <date>" (default 1 Nov 2025).
- Telegram and X archives run one source at a time, with 0107's waits; the Sources page can queue several and runs
  them in turn (never two Telegram or two Nitter jobs at once).

### D. Translation (0086 F5)
1. **On request:** a "Translate" button on any non-English post (post page, Mark X panel) adds a `translate` task;
   the next worker run answers it; the English shows under the original, marked "Translated by Haiku", with ✓ / ✗.
2. **Automatic for News leads:** a non-English post that leads a Mark X News row is planned for translation.
3. **The slang glossary** (F5's measured fix for the 2% misleading), in the guide (translate-v2), measured on F5's set.
4. Not in v10.5: translating every Russian post in the archive (the owner decides after D1–D3 are measured).

### E. Small, from the v10.4 builds
1. **Posts with no normalised address** (1,146 on the server copy, 7 sources, fetched 18–21 Mar 2026): a one-off,
   resumable repair that fills `normalized_url` by today's rule; an invariant that none is left. A database write:
   the deploy's first start runs it, logged.
2. **0128's questions, the defaults unless the owner says otherwise:** a leak "hours before" = within 24 h; a leak with
   its own coverage stays flagged, not split; the original first stays on while labels come in.
3. **The rumour judge** (0128 Q1, a new task kind linking a leak story to its release, about $0.001 a leak story): only
   if the owner says yes.

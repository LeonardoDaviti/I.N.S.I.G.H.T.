## Wrong in v10.0.1
1. **A check removes a post that links the story's own page.**
   - 5 of the 8 Mark IX rows Mark X drops from News are dropped wrongly. In each, Mark IX joined the post by a link to the same page as the other post, and Haiku answered "different" at 0.88–0.95 with a reason that names the same event in other words.
   - Over all removals and demotions: 6 right, 6 wrong, 2 debatable of 14.
   - 0112 Decided 8's second asking covers joins, pairs and merges, not checks.
2. **A row loses its Haiku headline** (19 of 124 rows, 6 of the 10 largest).
   - The page takes the headline from the row's first post's story (`markx/news.py`). When that post joined by name, its story has none, though the row's main story has one (5 of 19).
   - Headlines are asked only for stories of 2+ voices, so a row built from smaller stories never gets one (14 of 19).
   - 11 of 165 headline answers were refused: 9 for being one word over the 14 the guide asks for (`schemas.py` allows no slack, where readings get some), and 2 for a figure no member writes.
3. **Decision runs start at the wrong moment:**
   - one ran during Mark IX's first-start rebuild, at 0 of 49,384 posts, and queued 159 checks against stories about to change;
   - one started 29 s before the candidate finder it depends on had finished.
4. **The reading window applies late.** A change counts only once fewer than 20 tasks of that kind wait. With 500 planned at a time, it can wait up to 24 leases, and narrowing it never drops tasks already queued.
5. **A document's later analyses don't join its story** (the S-1 case). No candidate rule links an analysis of a filing posted days later, so no merge question is asked.
6. **The entities answer check** doesn't fold ’ and ' (`validate.py`), so it refuses valid names.
7. **The entity-candidate flush recomputes neighbours it already found:** 75 s, with 35 s of CPU, for 0 new candidates on a day's data.

## Decided
1. **Checks never remove a post Mark IX joined by a link to the story's page** (link, citation or quote to the page another member links, or to the origin). Such a check can only demote it to a mention. Removing a two-voice story's second voice is asked a second time, as 0112 Decided 8 does for joins.
2. **Headlines:**
   - a Mark X News row takes the headline of its main story (most voices), not its first post's;
   - every News row gets a headline task, whatever its stories' voices;
   - the headline check allows 2 words over the guide's 14, as readings get slack. Figures must still occur in a member.
3. **The order of runs:**
   - no decision run while Mark IX's story job holds its lock or a rule-change rebuild is in progress (it waits, then runs once);
   - a decision run triggered by vectors waits for the candidate finder of those vectors.

   Both go in 0112 Decided 12's scheduler, in `markx/jobs.py`.
4. **The reading window counts at once:**
   - a change of the setting re-plans on the next lease (the planner runs whenever the window it last planned differs from the setting, not only below 20 waiting);
   - narrowing it doesn't drop tasks already queued, but leases newest-first inside the window before older ones.
5. **A document as a merge candidate** (owner's call; default: yes): two stories whose members link the same document page (a filing, a paper, a report: the address shapes of 0106 and 0109) within 30 days become a `merge` candidate, never a rule.
6. **The answer check folds typographic apostrophes and quotes** (’ ‘ ” “ to ' and ") as it already folds dashes and ё.
7. **The entity-candidate flush is incremental:** it reads only posts embedded since its last run (0116's pattern).
8. **0112 Decided 13's check also runs during the first start's rebuild** (the v10.0.1 review saw 2.0–5.8 s stalls of the whole API process then; 0117's stack dump names them on the server).

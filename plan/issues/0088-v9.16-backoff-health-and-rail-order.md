# 0088 — v9.16: a wait that fits the error, health that names the site, and the rail's newest unread first

Status: **built, not merged** (2026-11-04, branch `issue-0086-backoff-and-rail`; filed as 0086, renumbered: another session holds 0086 and 0087). Item 8 of 0080, moved here, plus the reader rail's order. Small; merges on its own (the merge rule, 0075).

**Constraints:** download nothing; no real names in committed files or fixtures; nothing merges unless `scripts/realdata-check.sh` passes. Not News, the post page or Discover (other slices hold them).

## Why
1. **A 502 put a source to sleep for days.** On the server (2026-11-03), a feed service answering HTTP 502 put a source into "dormant · retry in 6d · 11 failures". From this machine the same address answered 200 (slow, 4–13 s).
   - The backoff ladder (`ingestion/schedule.py`: 1h, 2h, 4h … capped at 24h, and 7 days from the 10th failure in a row) treats every error alike. A 5xx or a timeout is the other server's trouble, usually over within hours, so a days-long wait only loses posts.
2. **The health text blames "the server".** The source sheet says "The server is failing (HTTP 502)." It doesn't say whose server: it reads as INSIGHT's.
3. **The rail's "Sources with unread" puts the biggest backlog first.** It sorts by unread count, so a source with 300 old unread posts stays on top while one that posted a minute ago sits low. The "By source" view already orders its groups by their newest post; the rail should agree.

## Decided (owner, 2026-11-04)
1. **A 5xx or a timeout retries within hours, never days.** Its backoff is the same ladder capped at 12 h (`INSIGHT_BACKOFF_SERVER_MAX_HOURS`), however many failures in a row, and the source is never "dormant" for it. Every other error keeps today's ladder; 401/403/404/410 handling stays as it is (other issues cover them).
   - "Dormant" means what it waits: a source is dormant when its wait is longer than the normal 24 h cap, not merely after 10 failures.
2. **The health text names the site:** "example.org is failing (HTTP 502): its own server didn't answer, usually temporary." (A timeout: "(no answer in time)".)
3. **The rail lists sources with unread by their newest unread post, newest first; ties by name.** The badge stays the unread count. The counts API gives each source's newest unread time (`newest_unread: {id: time}`, next to `by_source`).

## Built (2026-11-04)
1. **The wait.** `HttpError.their_trouble` (a 5xx or a timeout; the source doctor's `server_error` signal now reads it too). The runner passes it to `backoff_delay`, whose cap is then `BACKOFF_SERVER_MAX` (12 h, `INSIGHT_BACKOFF_SERVER_MAX_HOURS`) whatever the count.
   - Before: 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h, 16h, 24h …, and 7 days from the 10th failure in a row, for every error.
   - After: a 5xx or a timeout goes 1h, 2h, 4h, 8h, 12h, 12h … forever. Anything else (404/410/401/403, a parse error, a refused connection) is unchanged.
   - Jitter still only goes down (×0.85–1.0).
   - Health's `dormant` is now "10+ failures and a wait past the 24 h cap" (`next_retry_at − last_error_at`), so a 5xx source is never dormant.
   - A source already waiting days from before this change keeps that wait until its next attempt (Fetch now, or the wait ends). Nothing rewrites stored waits.
2. **The text.** The health row's `server_error` is `{status, site}`, with the site as `publication_of(host)`. The sheet reads "example.org is failing (HTTP 502): its own server didn't answer, usually temporary." On a timeout it reads "(no answer in time)". `pages/sources.md` says so.
3. **The rail.** `unread_counts` adds `newest_unread: {source: time}`. It is `MAX(COALESCE(surfaced_at, published_at, fetched_at))`, the same sort key "By source" groups by, taken in the same GROUP BY as the count. The rail sorts by it (newest first), ties by name; the badge is unchanged. `pages/reader.md` says so.
   - Cost, on a scratch copy of the lab (41,637 posts, 13,031 unread), EXPLAIN ANALYZE: the same plan before and after (a parallel seq scan, two anti-joins, a hash aggregate), 17–25 ms both, run to run.
   - Bundle (gzip): the feed route is 172,801 bytes after and 172,811 on `main` (0.97% / 0.98% of the 1% budget). The post route is 312,843 against 312,876.
4. **Tests.** The ladder's 12 h cap at 4, 5, 6, 10, 11 and 500 failures. The runner: a 502 and a timeout wait 12 h at the 11th failure, a 404 and a parse error 7 days. Health's `dormant` follows what it waits. The doctor's row names the site. The sheet's two texts. `newest_unread` ignores a newer read post. The rail puts a newer, smaller source above a bigger, older one, with ties by name.

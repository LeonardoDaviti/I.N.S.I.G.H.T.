# 0092 — Back in the news: an old story that gets a fresh burst reappears in News

Status: **future** (2026-11-04). Mark X, after Shadow's decisions (0086 E).

## Why
- **A story that comes back weeks later is lost today:** Mark IX's link window runs from a page's first linker (0089), so a later burst can't join, and News never shows it again.
- **On the lab copy:** 23 pages had a late burst, 47 pairs, 35 of them in no story together.
- **Labelled (experiment W):** 23 of the 35 burst pairs are one story or a follow-up, 3 use the page as background, and 9 are only the same topic or unrelated. So a burst can't be trusted on its own; the judge decides (0086's `burst` candidates).

## What
- **When it fires:**
  - 2+ new voices within 72 hours link an old story's page, or the judge calls 2+ new posts "same event" at the merge bar;
  - and the story's last post is 7+ days older.
- **The News row:** "Back in the news: <the story>", dated by the burst, with the new voices first and the original story one tap away.
- **What doesn't change:** the story keeps its own date and its old row (keep everything).
- **A follow-up isn't this:** it gets its own row, "Follows up: <the earlier story>" (0086's late-post table).

## Measure before adopting
- How many fire a month on a server copy.
- Your labels on the first 20: worth a News row or not.

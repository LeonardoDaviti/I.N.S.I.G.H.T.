# 0128 — Stealth v10.4 slice C: how Mark X stories are built (event type, leaks, the original first, published order, the entities stage's own budget)

Status: **built on branch `worktree-agent-ad2b9b753e8bcc086`, not merged** (2026-11-07). Mark X Stealth, in Shadow. Slice C of v10.4; slices A (search) and B (post page, post_refs, migration 0076) are other branches.

## Why
The owner decided how a story is built (0120 "What the owner decided", 0086 E's late-post table). Mark X shows none of it yet:
- a story has no event type;
- a leak is a member like any other, and a leak story never meets its release;
- the first post is the earliest one, not the original;
- Mark X orders some lines by when a post was received (`surfaced_at` first);
- the entities stage's full pass took 63 s of the decision run's 120 s limit on the server. One slow pass can stop the whole run.

## The owner's rules (decided; not re-asked)
- **A story is one happening.** A follow-up is its own story, linked.
- **A minor leak hours before the release joins the release.** A leak days earlier, with coverage of its own, is its own story, linked "came true".
- **The original is found by links and by maker,** and posts are ordered by published time, not received time. A saved page uses the page's own date.
- Mark X writes only `mx_*` tables; Mark IX is identical with Mark X on or off.

## Scope
**In:**
1. An event type per Mark X story, from its members' `read3` readings (0127). No new model kind.
2. Leaks inside a story and leak stories linked "came true" to their release, shown on both stories.
3. The original by code (0120's "The original by code", 0119 B2's port), shown as the story's first post.
4. Published order in Mark X's story lines.
5. The entities stage's own time budget, its full pass resumable across runs.
6. A check (no second fix): does (3) fix the perspectives weak spot of 0119 B's reader review?

**Out:**
- New Haiku kinds or asks. The lab's rumour judge (read_v3e: F1 0.80 behind a precise flag) is a new kind; not built here.
- `read3` for posts outside stories. A lone leak post has no `read3` reading, so it can't be flagged (below).
- Writing the leak join into `mx_story_posts`. The decision rules don't change (below).

## Decided (this slice's readings of the rules)

### 1. The story's event type (code, `markx/built.py`)
- The type of the story's news happening: `folds.story_type`, the most common of the 8 news types among members with a `read-v3` reading (a tie: the first to appear).
- Otherwise the most common type among the read members (a story of leaks is `leak_rumour`; of essays, `opinion_analysis`).
- None while no member is read.
- Shown with how many members were read ("model release · 5 of 6 read").
- **Not a bar-met feature:** on unseen lab posts the 14 types are 73.9% right per post (0127). The story's type is a vote over its members, so it should do better; this is unmeasured. It is labelled "experimental" on the page.

### 2. Leaks (code, `markx/built.py`)
- **A leak** is a member whose `read-v3` type is `leak_rumour` (lab, unseen: precision 100%, recall 56%).
- **The release** of a story is its earliest member of the story's news type that isn't a leak.

**Inside a story** (the judge already joined the leak):
- A leak member before the release is shown under "Before the release", with how long before.
- It **joins** (stays as a member) when it is at most `JOIN_HOURS` = 24 hours before the release, or when it is the only voice that leaked.
- When 2+ voices leaked it more than 24 hours before, the leak has coverage of its own. By the rule that's its own story, linked "came true". Mark X's decisions don't split stories by type yet, so the page says so ("its own story by the rule; not split yet") and counts these cases.

**Across stories ("came true"):**
- **A leak story:** a Mark X story whose event type is `leak_rumour`.
- **Linked to a release story** when all of these hold:
  - the release story's type is a news type;
  - its release is after the leak story's first post, and within `CAME_TRUE_DAYS` = 30 days;
  - Mark X already has a judged connection between them (development, follows up, same event; connect-v1, 0086 F6).
  - **Built but off:** a shared **main subject** (an entity named as `subject` by the most members, by identity's roots, 0119 A; or one's subject the other's family, never its maker). Measured on the lab it linked 0 of 5 right (below), so `SUBJECT_LINKS` is off.
- **If the leak story has one voice and its first post is at most 24 hours before the release,** it is shown in the release story as a leak that joins ("hours before, no coverage of its own").
- **Otherwise it stays its own story,** linked both ways: "Came true: <the release>" on the leak story, "Leaked earlier: <the leak>" on the release story.
- A leak story may link to several releases; each release takes its nearest-before leak stories.

**Why code, not a judge:** the lab's code rules for rumour → confirmation found nothing (round 1: 0/6 with read-v3 and shared words; Mark IX 0/6). Only a new pairwise judge worked (round 2, F1 0.80). This slice tried identity's entities instead of words. They didn't do better (Built), so the judge is the next step, at a stated cost, for the owner to approve.

**Not written into `mx_story_posts`:** the join and the links are computed when the page is built, from rows Mark X already keeps (readings, memberships, mentions, connections), as the fold is (0127 §3). So:
- the decisions, News's voices and `markx.ix_unchanged` are unchanged;
- nothing derived needs closing (keep everything);
- writing them as memberships and connections is a change to the decision rules, to be measured on its own.

### 3. The original first (code, reusing `perspectives.Story`)
- **Order of precedence:**
  - a member whose own address is the page most members link to (links);
  - else the maker's own post (its source is the maker or the maker's, 0119 B2);
  - else a page-kind member on the maker's page;
  - else a short link post carrying the maker's page.
  - The earliest of a kind wins.
- **No member is the original:** the page members link is shown as "Source page" (0120 step 2), and the first post is the earliest.
- **Copies** (same source, same text) count as their first.
- **Shown first:**
  - on the Mark X News row: the original's line moves to the top, marked "original · by links / by its maker";
  - on the story page: the same line above "Worth opening".
- `perspectives.stage` itself is not changed: its reference and inputs are the frozen port's (0119 B), so nothing is asked again.

### 4. Published order
- **Mark X's "published":** `COALESCE(published_at, surfaced_at, fetched_at)`.
  - `published_at` is a saved page's own date (0063) and a feed post's date.
  - `surfaced_at` (when a page was put in front of the owner, 0039) only stands in for a post with no date.
- **Used for:** the order and times of a News row's lines, the fold's order and head (`folds.story_folds`, `story_view`), and the story page.
- **Not changed:** the windows (a post received in the last 30 days still counts as recent), the decision rules' `at`, and every task input. A changed input would be a new question.
- On the server copy only 2 of 2,469 current members have a received time different from their date.

### 5. The entities stage's own budget
- **Today:** the decision run has one limit, `LIMIT` = 120 s, counted across every stage. Past it, the run stops before its next stage and its one transaction rolls back: nothing is written, the stories included. The entities stage's full pass (nightly, every mention relinked, every entity pooled) took 63 s on the server.
- **Decided:**
  - **The entities stage gets its own budget,** `ENTITY_BUDGET` = 45 s. Its time doesn't count towards the run's 120 s, so it can't starve the other stages; `HARD_LIMIT` (900 s) still kills a run that never ends.
  - **The full pass is resumable:**
    - it pools and decides the entities in chunks of `SEED_CHUNK` (1,000, in id order), after linking every mention;
    - past its budget it stops after a chunk and the run goes on;
    - the run's counts keep where it stopped (`entities.full = {done: false, after: <last entity id>, started: …}`), and the next run continues from there: it links the new mentions, then the rest of the chunks;
    - the pass is done when the last chunk is; the night's full pass counts as done only then (`full_due`);
    - collisions run when it is done.
  - **The incremental pass** (every other run, about 0.5 s) is unchanged, inside the same budget.
- **Chunks are seed sets,** as incremental passes already are. The chain guard sees the merges of earlier chunks through the book, so a chunked pass decides as a series of incremental passes would. Auto merges are ordered by confidence within a chunk, not across the whole pool.
